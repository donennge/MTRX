Utilities

Practical tools, running in your browser.

Everything below runs client-side. Files are not uploaded anywhere. The encryption uses the Web Crypto API; the obfuscators are real source transformations, not formatting tricks.

These utilities are for developers who need to encrypt a file, protect a script, or look at something they did not write. Each tool is independent and works on its own.
